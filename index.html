Average is the third rating from the bottom in a 5-point scale: Incomplete → Poor → Average → Good → Excellent. It means the asset group has the minimum required assets but lacks diversity, variety and quality depth that Google needs to serve ads optimally across all its inventory types.

Landing Page Rules — Non-Negotiable for CRO

Message Match — Every Page
The H1 of every landing page must exactly match the headline of the ad that sent the user there. "Mobile Roadworthy Certificate — Brisbane North" in the ad = "Mobile Roadworthy Certificate — Brisbane North" as the H1. Google scores landing page relevance by comparing ad copy to page content. Mismatch = lower Quality Score = higher CPC.
Price Above the Fold — Always
Auto King's fixed pricing ($130 RWC, $149 Pink Slip, $220 Servicing, $299 PPI) is a competitive advantage. Hiding the price forces customers to search for it — some will bounce. Display the price prominently near the CTA button as a trust signal, not as a deterrent. "Flat $130 — No Hidden Fees" converts better than price ambiguity.
5 Form Fields — Maximum
Name, Phone, Postcode, Service Needed, Best Contact Time. Plus one hidden GCLID field. Every additional visible field reduces form conversion rate by approximately 5–10%. Do not ask for: Vehicle make, Vehicle rego, Address, Email (call-back model doesn't need it), Notes. The mechanic can ask for details when they call back.
Unique Thank-You URL Per Service
/thank-you-roadworthy · /thank-you-pink-slip · /thank-you-servicing · /thank-you-prepurchase · /thank-you-diagnosis · /thank-you-repairs. GTM cannot assign service-specific conversion values without distinct confirmation URLs. Without this, a $299 pre-purchase lead is tracked identically to a $130 roadworthy lead — killing value-based bidding.
Scarcity & Urgency — Without Lying
Real urgency exists in Auto King's model — there genuinely are limited slots per region per day. "Only 3 slots available today in Brisbane North" is verifiable and drives action. Countdown timers for same-day availability work for service businesses. Do not fabricate scarcity — use real operational constraints.
Platform Choice — Abandon Instapage
Instapage crashed once and was never rebuilt. Use Unbounce ($99–249/month) or Swipe Pages ($39–89/month) — both have AMP support (faster mobile load), native GTM integration, and duplicate-page functionality for building 18+ service×geo combinations efficiently. Every page inherits the same GTM container.

Monday.com → Google Ads Offline Conversion Pipeline

Step 1 — Capture GCLID at form submit
Add a hidden field to every landing page form: <input type="hidden" name="gclid" id="gclid_field"> with JavaScript that captures the gclid URL parameter on page load. This field submits with the form, invisibly connecting every web lead to its Google Ads click.
Step 2 — Store GCLID in Monday.com
Add a "gclid" text column to the Monday.com leads board. Configure the form integration to map the hidden gclid field to this column. Confirm it is populating by testing a form submission with a manual GCLID parameter in the URL.
Step 3 — Push booked jobs back to Ads
Zapier workflow: Monday item status changes to "Booked" → Zapier reads GCLID + service type + job value → Sends to Google Ads Offline Conversion Upload API. Revenue now flows back to the originating campaign, ad group, and keyword. Smart bidding optimises to actual booked revenue.
